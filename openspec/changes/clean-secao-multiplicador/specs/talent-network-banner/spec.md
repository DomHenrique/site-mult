## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Institutional Talent Network Banner
The system SHALL display an integrated, lightweight secondary banner for recruiting partner engineers and specialists within the Mult network, without any references to internal welcome kits or physical gifts.

#### Scenario: Visual presentation of the banner
- **WHEN** a visitor navigates to the `#multiplicador` section on the homepage
- **THEN** the system displays a clean, professional banner featuring an eyebrow badge, a clear headline, descriptive text inviting partner specialists, and a CTA button
- **AND** the banner visual blends seamlessly into the section background without harsh borders or floating rectangular boxes
- **AND** no references to welcome kits, mugs, badges, or onboarding gifts are displayed

### Requirement: Prospective Specialist Narrative
The textual copy in the banner SHALL use future and invitation verb tenses aimed at attracting external specialists, avoiding internal employee onboarding phrasing.

#### Scenario: External audience messaging
- **WHEN** the visitor reads the copy in the `#multiplicador` section
- **THEN** the text addresses the visitor as a prospective partner specialist (e.g., "Conecte seu conhecimento...", "Faça parte da nossa rede")
- **AND** the text conveys the opportunity to collaborate on high-impact engineering projects across Brazil

### Requirement: Contextual CTA Navigation
The banner CTA button SHALL direct the user to the contact form with pre-selected context for specialist partnership.

#### Scenario: User clicks on the talent network CTA
- **WHEN** the user clicks the "Candidate-se à Rede Mult" button
- **THEN** the page smoothly scrolls to the `#contato` section
- **AND** the contact form subject or context field is populated with "Rede de Especialistas"
